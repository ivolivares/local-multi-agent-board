# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in **Local Multi-Agent Board** a harassment-free, welcoming, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Focus feedback on code, architecture, and user experience rather than individuals.
- Credit prior art, specifications, and upstream open-source libraries clearly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Malicious code, hidden telemetry, or deceptive contributions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct. To report concerns, contact the maintainer via [iolivares.com](https://iolivares.com).
